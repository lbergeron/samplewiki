# Surfing Fundamentals: How to Paddle, Pop Up, and Stand Properly

Learning on land first makes your time in the water safer and more productive. Focus on balance, timing, and control rather than standing on every wave.

## Before entering the water

- Check the conditions and choose a spot suited to your experience.
- Use a board with enough volume to float you comfortably.
- Attach and inspect your leash.
- Warm up your shoulders, hips, and legs.
- Review local safety and right-of-way rules.

## Paddle efficiently

Lie centered on the board with the nose just clear of the water. If the nose points high, move forward; if it dives, move slightly back. Keep your legs together and use long, relaxed strokes close to the rails.

When paddling for a wave, turn toward the beach early, look over your shoulder, and increase effort as the wave reaches you. Avoid frantic, short strokes that waste energy and destabilize the board.

## Practice the pop-up

Practice on a dry, stable surface first. Start lying down with your hands near your ribs.

1. Press your chest up while keeping your hands planted.
2. Bring your front foot between your hands.
3. Bring the back foot forward into a comfortable stance.
4. Rise with your hips low and knees bent.
5. Keep your shoulders and hips facing across the board.

Move in one controlled motion and keep your head up.

## Find a balanced stance

Most surfers use either a left-foot-forward regular stance or a right-foot-forward goofy stance. Try both on land and use the one that feels more stable.

- Keep feet roughly shoulder-width apart.
- Bend both knees instead of standing tall.
- Keep your weight centered between your feet.
- Look where you want to go.
- Keep your arms relaxed for balance.

## Common mistakes

- Standing before the board is moving down the wave.
- Looking at your feet instead of the open face.
- Using straight legs and a stiff upper body.
- Paddling too far forward or back.
- Attempting waves that are too large or crowded.

## Practice checklist

- [ ] I can paddle while keeping the board level.
- [ ] I can pop up smoothly on land.
- [ ] I can identify my comfortable stance.
- [ ] I know how to fall away from the board.
