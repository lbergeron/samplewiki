# Surfing Glossary

## A–C

- **A-frame:** A peak that breaks both left and right.
- **Backside:** Surfing with your back toward the wave face.
- **Beach break:** Waves breaking over a sandy bottom.
- **Bottom turn:** The first turn at the bottom of the wave, used to redirect up the face.
- **Channel:** A deeper area where water or currents move, often providing an easier paddle-out.
- **Closeout:** A wave that breaks across its entire length without a usable shoulder.
- **Cutback:** A turn back toward the breaking part of the wave.

## D–L

- **Deck:** The top surface of a surfboard.
- **Drop in:** Taking off on a wave in front of a surfer who has priority.
- **Duck dive:** Pushing a shortboard and yourself under an approaching wave.
- **Face:** The unbroken, sloping surface of a wave.
- **Fin:** A foil attached under a board that provides hold and control.
- **Glassy:** A smooth surface with little or no wind texture.
- **Goofy:** A stance with the right foot forward.
- **Green wave:** An unbroken wave, as opposed to whitewater.
- **Inside:** The area closer to shore or farther from the main peak.
- **Leash:** The cord connecting a surfer to the board.
- **Lineup:** The area where surfers wait for waves.

## M–R

- **Nose:** The front of a surfboard.
- **Offshore wind:** Wind blowing from land toward the ocean; it can hold up or groom waves.
- **Onshore wind:** Wind blowing from the ocean toward land; it can make waves choppy.
- **Peak:** The first or highest part of a wave to break.
- **Point break:** A wave breaking along a headland, reef, or sand point.
- **Priority:** The right to catch a wave under local etiquette rules.
- **Regular:** A stance with the left foot forward.
- **Reef break:** A wave breaking over rock, coral, or another firm bottom.
- **Rip current:** A narrow current moving away from shore.

## S–W

- **Set:** A group of waves arriving together.
- **Shoulder:** The unbroken section beside the breaking part of a wave.
- **Swell:** Energy moving through the ocean that can form surf.
- **Takeoff:** The moment a surfer catches and begins riding a wave.
- **Trim:** Adjusting position and weight to maintain speed along the wave.
- **Turtle roll:** Rolling a longboard upside down to pass under a wave.
- **Whitewater:** Broken, foamy water after a wave breaks.
- **Wipeout:** Falling or being knocked off the board during a ride.
