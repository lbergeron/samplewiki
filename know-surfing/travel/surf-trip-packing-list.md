# Surf Trip Packing List

Pack for the waves you expect, but include enough everyday clothing and basic repair supplies for delays, flat days, and changing weather.

## Surf equipment

- Surfboard, fins, fin key, and leash.
- Board bag, board socks, and padding.
- Wetsuit, boots, gloves, or hood as conditions require.
- Wax suited to the water temperature.
- Rash guard, changing towel, and dry bags.
- Small repair supplies appropriate to your board.

If renting equipment, confirm what is included, the available sizes, and the rental shop’s damage policy.

## Clothing and personal items

- Quick-drying surf and beach clothes.
- Warm layers and rain protection.
- Sun protection, hat, and reusable water bottle.
- Toiletries, medications, and basic first-aid supplies.
- Chargers, adapters, and a waterproof phone pouch.

Avoid relying on one item that may be delayed with checked luggage. Keep essential medication and documents with you.

## Documents and planning

- Passport, visas, and booking confirmations.
- Travel and activity insurance details.
- Emergency contacts and accommodation address.
- Carrier rules and surfboard baggage receipts.
- Copies of important documents stored securely.

## Protect and organize gear

Label bags with your contact information. Keep fins and small hardware in a dedicated pouch. Photograph boards and valuable equipment before travel, and list serial numbers when relevant.

Do not pack wet equipment in a closed bag for longer than necessary. Rinse, drain, and dry it at the destination.

## Final checklist

- [ ] I checked the destination forecast and water temperature.
- [ ] My board bag fits the carrier’s size and weight limits.
- [ ] Fins and tools are packed securely.
- [ ] Essential documents and medication are in my carry-on.
- [ ] I have insurance and emergency contacts.
- [ ] I know where I can rent, repair, or replace equipment.
