# Tips for Travelling with Your Surfboard

Transport damage often happens during handling rather than surfing. Plan the journey, use suitable protection, and check carrier rules before leaving.

## Choose the right bag

Use a padded travel bag with enough room for the board and protective padding. A bag should not be so tight that fins, rails, or nose press against the fabric.

For multiple boards, separate them with towels or board socks and add padding around rails, noses, tails, and fin boxes.

## Prepare the board

Before packing:

- Clean and dry the board.
- Remove or protect fins and store hardware in a labeled pouch.
- Protect the nose, tail, rails, and leash plug.
- Remove wax if heat or a long journey could cause it to melt.
- Photograph the board and note existing damage.

Avoid leaving the board sealed in a hot vehicle or bag for long periods.

## Check airline or carrier rules

Policies vary by carrier, route, board count, bag size, weight, and season. Check the current rules and fees directly with the carrier before booking.

Confirm whether fins, wetsuits, and other gear can share the bag and whether the bag is treated as oversized luggage.

## During the journey

Label the bag with your contact information and destination. Keep essential items such as medication, documents, and a change of clothes separate from the board bag.

When loading a vehicle, keep the board away from heavy objects and sharp edges. On roof racks, use appropriate straps, protective padding, and a secure tie-down method.

## On arrival

Inspect the bag before accepting or leaving the baggage area when possible. Open it soon afterward and check the board, fin boxes, leash plug, and rails. Photograph new damage and report it promptly under the carrier’s process.

Let a board acclimatize away from direct heat before sealing it in a bag.

## Travel checklist

- [ ] I checked the carrier’s current surfboard policy.
- [ ] The board is padded at vulnerable points.
- [ ] Fins and hardware are packed separately.
- [ ] The bag is labeled and within size and weight limits.
- [ ] I photographed the board before departure.
